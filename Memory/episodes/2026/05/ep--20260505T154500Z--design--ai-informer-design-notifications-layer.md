## Reflection

Three load-bearing decisions worked out: (1) Choosing the companion-file path for the notification design (design/notifications.md, 49 lines) rather than inlining into main design §6 was correct — main stayed at 63/100 with three small surgical edits, while the companion file got room to spell out the adapter pattern and extensibility recipe without budget pressure. (2) Diverging from the reference impl's env-var pattern toward a config-file source of truth was the right call: it makes the URL discoverable (file in repo) rather than buried in environment, supports named channels (the human asked for `--channel <name>`), and keeps the single-authoring invariant grep-checkable. The trade-off was surfaced to the human in the mailbox reply rather than silently chosen — appropriate for a design-call decision. (3) Refusing the planner's slugged path form (`08-urgency`) and keeping the existing short numeric form (`08`) preserved intra-file path consistency: Stage 4 references output.md from the same per-run dir Stage 1 wrote into. A pure planner-following executor would have introduced a path mismatch. The lesson for future planning: when path forms appear in handoff briefs, the planner should defer to existing in-file conventions and only specify path forms when the convention is being newly established. Two distillation candidates noted by evaluator (a heuristic about authoring-meta leaks in design files, and a skill candidate around credential containment + grep verification) are below promotion thresholds and recorded only.
