## Reflection

Three calls worth recording: (1) The single-urlopen-seam discipline in FeishuChannel.send made the unit test trivially clean — the test monkeypatches one symbol and exercises every dispatch path without any real network. The lesson generalizes: when authoring an HTTP-bearing module under a no-live-test constraint, design the HTTP boundary as a single explicit seam in the adapter, not as a fan of helper functions. Anchoring at __file__ is the simple correct choice for a CLI module that may be invoked from anywhere; the CWD fallback covers the rare case of out-of-tree installation. (3) Trimming cli.py from 104 to 88 lines via docstring compaction + helper consolidation + inline channel lookup preserved every load-bearing behavior while staying under the 100-line cap. The trim cycle is now routinized across four consecutive build episodes (AGENTS.md hit 150 cap, helper hit 80 cap, this hit 100 cap) — future similar episodes should expect the trim cycle as a routine response to caps, not a sign of failure.
